OPEN QUESTION routed to the implementer before we build on this: why does a worker miss a collection? If it is an edge race at registration or teardown, the marker is the whole fix. If the worker misses collections while busy (plan 2 task 8 sampled only on CollectTelemetry, and the flush interleaves on the same thread), telemetry goes blind under load and that is a PRODUCT finding for the bounded-remediation task, not a harness one. Asked for the answered-versus-served counts and where the gaps fall relative to flush activity.
Two candidate mechanisms identified from source: (1) an inherent per-collection race -- the pipeline control loop updates and reports its own metrics including the uptime gauge and only then dispatches collect-telemetry to the nodes at the end of the same tick, so a scrape landing between those points sees advanced uptime with no exporter sample; (2) deferral -- dispatch to a node is deliberately non-blocking and a full control channel pushes the message to a pending queue for a later iteration, so a node under pressure has its collection deferred.
PRE-REGISTERED (decided before seeing the data): uniform misses shorter than one interval = the race, harness-only, liveness marker is the whole fix plus a documented rule that assertions span several collections. Misses clustered against flush windows = deferral, a PRODUCT finding routed to bounded remediation, where the candidates to weigh are a cheap periodic self-sample inside the worker (distinct from the per-turn sampling plan 2 removed for being O(N^2) in live tokens), a larger control channel, or accept-and-document. Both present: report shares, treat the clustered part as the product finding. The miss RATE must be recorded as a number, because it determines how many collections an assertion must span; if it is material the plan's assertion windows widen before the soak runs.
